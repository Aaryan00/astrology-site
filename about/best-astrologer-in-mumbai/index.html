About Shri Shri Amit Agarwal

A Top Astrologer Trusted by Mumbai Clients

Shri Shri Amit Agarwal is internationally recognised as one of the world's most trusted, most accurate and highly recommended celebrity Astrologers, Vastu Consultants, Palmists and Numerologists. Based in Kanpur, India, he consults clients in Mumbai and across South Mumbai, Andheri, Bandra, Thane and Navi Mumbai through video and phone sessions.

Beyond the esoteric sciences, he is a celebrated Motivational Speaker, Life Coach and Writer. He serves as Managing Director of Home Food Products, Charter President of Rotary Club Kanpur Global, and Trustee of Baldau Mandir Trust.

Honoured with the Durga Swaroopa Samman 2025, the Icons of Bharat Award 2025 and the National Pride & Excellence Award, his blend of Vedic wisdom and humanitarian service has earned him a following across 25+ countries. Consultations are conducted in Hindi, English and Marathi.
